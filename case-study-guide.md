# AI use-case template

Inclusive research discovery, contribution, and recognition

1. Use-case name
A short description of what AI enables.

2. Target users
Who benefits? For example, citizen scientists, independent researchers, small companies, community research organizations, academic researchers, or funders.

3. User problem
What are people unable to find, contribute, connect, validate, or communicate today?

4. Desired outcome
What should become easier or possible?

5. Data inputs
Which information sources does the system need?
Research publications and reports.
Datasets and metadata.
Researcher profiles and credentials.
Blogs, videos, and other non-traditional research outputs.
Technical documentation and project descriptions.
Historical archives and community knowledge, subject to permissions.

6. AI capabilities
Which capabilities are required?
Semantic search and discovery.
Contextual classification.
Researcher and contribution identification.
Evidence extraction and synthesis.
Research quality and provenance support.
Recommendations and collaboration matching.
Translation and accessible content generation.

7. Human oversight
Who validates the outputs, approves publication, corrects errors, and challenges decisions?

8. Governance and safeguards
What permissions, access controls, privacy protections, attribution requirements, and appeal mechanisms are needed?

9. Benefits
What changes for the researcher, the research community, and the broader public?

10. Success measures
How will the organization demonstrate that discovery, participation, research quality, and equity have improved?
